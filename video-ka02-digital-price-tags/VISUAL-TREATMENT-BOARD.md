# Visual Treatment Board — First Pass

This is a beat-level board for story approval, not the final scene build. Each scene will later be
split into VO-anchored subsegments and must meet the production asset floor.

## Variety Targets

- At least 6 presentation registers represented.
- No more than 3 consecutive scenes with the same dominant register.
- One quick-cut store-work montage in Scene 02.
- One held 4–6 second breath as the study result lands in Scene 06.
- At least 90% denotative visuals; source captures under 35% of runtime.

## Visual Risks

| Scene | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 03–04 | Abstract boxes replace real life | Keep diagram over moving real store bed and return to a shopper every 10–15 seconds |
| 06 | A near-zero chart can look like “nothing happened” and lose energy | Make the correction itself the dramatic event; hold long enough to read |
| 07 | Viewers may merge online variation with Walmart's physical labels | Persistent `ONLINE PLATFORM — NOT SHELF LABEL` boundary and disconnected diagram edge |
| 08 | Data categories can become a scary unsupported montage | Every input appears only as it is named in the FTC document |
| 09 | Document fatigue | Use documents as proof flashes; carry meaning through the already-familiar system spine |
| 11 | Policy checklist feels detached | Resolve each guardrail through one shopper or worker consequence |
