# Visual Treatment Board — Approved Direction

This is the approved beat-level direction, not the final scene build. Each scene will be split into
VO-anchored subsegments and must meet the production asset floor. Rendered execution remains subject
to scene-level review and QC.

## Timing and Evidence Contract

- **Five-second ceiling:** most individual clips, crops, or completed presentation states run 2–5
  seconds. No ordinary visual may remain materially unchanged for more than 5 seconds.
- **A meaningful change counts:** a new real clip, source crop, camera reframe, data transition,
  diagram build, comparison state, or evidence highlight. Ambient particles or an imperceptible
  drift do not excuse a stale foreground.
- **Evidence exception:** a document may remain visible longer than 5 seconds when the viewer needs
  to read it or when the VO is quoting or closely paraphrasing it. The composition must crop to the
  relevant passage, keep the source identity visible, and continue a restrained camera move.
- **Synchronized highlighting:** the exact clause being read or paraphrased receives a warm-amber
  highlight as the corresponding VO begins. The highlight leads the spoken phrase by roughly
  0.2–0.5 seconds, remains through the phrase, then yields to the next passage. Unspoken text stays
  dimmed but legible enough to preserve document context.
- **No false proof:** highlighting may never join separate words, omit a qualifier, or visually imply
  that a source says more than it does. Company claims remain labeled as company claims; proposals
  remain labeled as proposed.
- **HyperFrames owns final composition:** real footage and source captures supply reality and proof;
  HyperFrames supplies cropping, depth, annotation, highlighting, diagrams, data visualization,
  transitions, and the persistent three-layer spine.

## Variety Targets

- At least 6 presentation registers represented.
- No more than 3 consecutive scenes with the same dominant register.
- One quick-cut store-work montage in Scene 02.
- One held 4–6 second breath as the study result lands in Scene 06.
- At least 90% denotative visuals; source captures under 35% of runtime.
- At least one material visual change every 5 seconds outside the evidence exception above.

| 09 | Document fatigue | Use documents as proof flashes; carry meaning through the already-familiar system spine |
