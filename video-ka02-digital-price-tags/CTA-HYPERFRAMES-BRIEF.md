# Scene 12 HyperFrames Brief — KeyAdvances CTA

Status: approved direction; exact cue times lock to the recorded Scene 12 transcript.

## Scene idea

Turn the episode's resolved three-layer spine into a short, satisfying participation sequence. The
CTA should feel like the final consequence of the story—not a generic YouTube template pasted after
it.

## Main visual

The active `PUBLIC LABEL → STORE PRICE ENGINE → CUSTOMER DATA` diagram simplifies into the
KeyAdvances wordmark. Its existing nodes then become the interaction controls: a price-tag-shaped
LIKE control, a SHARE path, and a notification bell. The final signal opens three human-scale windows
labeled `LIFE`, `WORK`, and `BUSINESS` before the channel promise lands.

## Design and motion

- Canvas: 1920×1080, 30 fps; all essential copy stays within a centered 1344×756 action-safe area.
- Palette: KeyAdvances midnight navy/charcoal, cyan for the newly possible, warm amber for the human
  consequence, cream only for the final promise.
- Type: mobile-readable; no CTA label below 60 px at 1920×1080.
- Motion: path travel, shape transformation, snap, and one controlled bell ring. Fades may support
  transitions but may not be the only motion.
- Audio accents: one quiet activation click, one share-path sweep, and one bell tone under narration.
- No visual state remains materially unchanged for more than 5 seconds.
- Final hold: 2–3 seconds, leaving clean space for YouTube end-screen elements if used.

## QA

- The visuals must follow the spoken order; never show all four asks simultaneously.
- The CTA must remain legible on a phone at normal YouTube viewing size.
- HyperFrames lint, transition-layout checks, rendered frame inspection, static-hold scan, and
  duration/frame-count verification are required before approval.
