# KeyAdvances Scene 06 Design

## Concept

The scene lives inside a **documentary evidence table**, not a dashboard. Real store imagery is
always faintly present underneath paper, ink, and illuminated shelf-label elements. The visual
argument begins with scale and anxiety, then becomes quieter and more precise as the result lands.

## Palette

- Midnight navy `#071522` — documentary wash and deep frame
- Ink `#111C25` — paper typography
- Warm paper `#F3EFE4` — evidence surface
- Signal cyan `#50D5E6` — technology, measured data, confirmed connection
- Evidence amber `#F2B84B` — caveats, attribution, human judgment
- Alert coral `#F16C5B` — feared surge path only; never the dominant color

## Typography

- Display: **Archivo Black**, uppercase, compact and declarative
- Evidence/body: **IBM Plex Mono**, tabular and source-like
- If local fonts are unavailable, fall back to `Arial Black` and `Courier New` without network loading.

## Focal Element

An oversized evidence card with a thin illuminated shelf-label edge. It can behave as a paper,
chart, map, or comparison surface without becoming generic software UI.

## Anchors

- Upper-left: small `KEYADVANCES / EVIDENCE 06` locator
- Lower-left: persistent source/limitation line
- Lower-right: subtle three-layer spine indicator, used only in the final interpretation beat

## Background Treatment

Use a real Walmart interior frame with a slow, continuous push. Desaturate and darken it through
the canonical media-treatment/color-grade path, then place a translucent navy scrim above it.
The image must remain identifiable as a working grocery aisle; it is atmosphere, not wallpaper.

## Motion Grammar

- Paper **pushes** or **slides**; it does not float.
- Data **assembles**, **counts**, **draws**, and **locks**.
- Caveats **stamp** onto the evidence and remain attached.
- The result beat deliberately reduces motion for a five-second reading breath.
- Handoffs use a brisk directional wipe or hard editorial cut; no decorative shader transition.
