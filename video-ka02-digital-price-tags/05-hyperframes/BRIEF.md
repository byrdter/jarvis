## Intent

Build Scene 06 first as the production prototype for the KeyAdvances episode about digital
price tags. The sequence should initially make 180 million observations feel enormous, then
let the actual evidence reverse the viewer's expectation. The tone is serious but lightly
presented: clear, calm, and memorable rather than ominous.

## Customizations

- Use real footage and real evidence as the visual foundation; use no photoreal AI.
- Keep normal visual states at or below five seconds, except a deliberate 4–6 second evidence-reading hold.
- Highlight the evidence language in synchronization with the narration.
- Use a faded real Walmart/store image beneath every designed state; never fall back to flat black.
- Make “180 million” and “114 stores / four states” memorable without drawing a misleading percentage chart.

## Notes

- The study is a working paper covering one unnamed grocery chain; keep both limitations visible.
- The research summary reports 0.005% before and 0.0056% after. Display both on a common scale and label the difference as statistically insignificant rather than visually exaggerating it.
- Attribute the research to Ioannis Stamatopoulos, Robert Sanders, and Robert Bray; identify the working-paper status.
- Walmart press footage permission is pending and is not required for this prototype.
