# Storyblocks and Pexels Download Brief

This is Terry's download checklist for the KeyAdvances digital-price-tag episode. It is deliberately
weighted toward actions we cannot establish with generic supermarket footage. Do not spend the
Storyblocks allowance on many versions of carts, exteriors, drone shots, or people merely walking.

## What to Download

Download **two good horizontal alternatives** for each action when available.

### Priority 3 — Only If the First Two Groups Are Covered

- One clean Walmart exterior or sign shot.
- One ordinary supermarket entrance.
- One cart-level aisle move.
- One produce establishing shot.
- One checkout-wide shot.

Pexels already supplies credible candidates for these generic needs, so they are the lowest-value use
of Storyblocks downloads.

## Target Batch

Aim for **25–35 distinct clips**:

- 12–16 exact worker/label/markdown actions;
- 10–14 shopper, checkout, produce, and online-shopping actions;
- no more than 3–5 establishing shots.

Long continuous clips are useful only when they contain multiple genuinely different camera setups.
Do not download five near-identical takes from the same shoot unless the action is one of the exact
Priority 1 gaps.

## Technical Selection Rules

- Horizontal **16:9** or a horizontal composition with safe room for a 16:9 crop.
- **4K preferred; 1920×1080 minimum.** Avoid vertical-only footage for the long-form master.
- Prefer **8–30 seconds**, stable or deliberately moving, at normal speed.
- Download the highest-quality original MP4 or MOV. Keep native frame rate; do not transcode.
- Prefer neutral/clean color over heavily stylized teal-orange grades.
- Avoid baked-in titles, watermarks, music, transitions, or split screens.
- Avoid clips whose central product price is obviously outside the U.S. context unless the framing can
  be cropped to a universal action.
- Avoid conspicuous retailer branding unless the clip is intentionally being used as that retailer.
- Avoid theatrical shock, worried-phone acting, hacker screens, surveillance cameras, and empty
  server-room imagery. They add mood but misstate the evidence.
- For recognizable people or private property, favor assets marked model/property released.
- Choose assets marked for commercial use under the applicable plan; do not choose `Editorial Only`
  material for the monetized episode without a separately documented clearance decision.

## Rights Records to Save With the Batch

For Storyblocks, save:

1. the filename and asset page URL;
2. the asset ID and contributor;
3. download date;
4. a screenshot or PDF of the plan/license in force on that date;
5. whether the asset says `Editorial Only`, `Model Released`, and `Property Released`;
6. the Storyblocks download-history record if available.

Storyblocks says its active-plan agreement is the proof of licensing and that recognizable people or
property may carry explicit release indicators. Keeping this material locally makes a future YouTube
claim much easier to answer.

For Pexels, save the asset page URL, creator, download date, and a capture of the Pexels license page.
