## Claims deliberately constrained

| rejected wording | script wording | reason |
|---|---|---|
| Walmart can change every price in minutes | A price-change job that took days can now be completed in minutes | “Every” is not established |
| Digital labels enable personalized prices | Individualized prices require the price system plus a data/delivery layer | The public label alone cannot personalize |
| The study proves ESLs never cause surge pricing | The one-chain working paper found virtually no adoption-driven surge pricing | Scope and publication status |
| Instacart used personal data to charge more | Randomized price variation was found; no demographic-causation evidence was found | Personal-data use not established |
| States are banning digital labels | NJ paused new use for one year; NYC measures remain proposed | Preserve law/proposal distinction |
