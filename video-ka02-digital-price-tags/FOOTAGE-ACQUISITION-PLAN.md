# Footage Acquisition Plan

This plan starts the visual build with real footage and real documents. HyperFrames is the
presentation layer—not a substitute for reality—and AI-generated imagery is reserved for generic,
non-evidentiary connective material only when a real or authored alternative cannot be found.

## Locked Mix and Editorial Rules

- **55–65% real moving footage:** stores, shoppers, workers, labels, hearings, and other real events.
- **20–30% real documents and stills:** primary-source pages, studies, laws, diagrams, and licensed
  photographs, animated through crops and synchronized highlights.
- **10–15% authored HyperFrames:** the three-layer spine, charts, transitions, comparison cards, and
  Scene 12 CTA.
- **0–5% AI generation:** generic props or atmosphere only; never a person, store, document, price,
  product feature, or event presented as evidence.
- Most individual clips or completed presentation states last **2–5 seconds**. Evidence documents may
  remain longer while the exact spoken passage is highlighted.
- Outside clips are single-use unless an explicit editorial reason requires a callback.
- Corporate footage proves that a product and workflow exist; it does not independently validate the
  company's claims. Scrutiny is carried by primary documents, government proceedings, independent
  research, and clearly attributed reporting.

## Rights Tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Production Rule |
|---|---|---|
| GREEN | Public domain, compatible Creative Commons, owned, or licensed for this use | May acquire after recording source, creator, license, and attribution |
| AMBER | Permission required, uncertain terms, or narrowly defensible critical quotation | Do not place in a final render until the basis for use is recorded |
| RED | Prohibited, no credible license, or unacceptable factual/identity risk | Do not acquire or use |

Walmart's media-library policy licenses its assets to accredited professional news organizations for
related news coverage. KeyAdvances should not assume it qualifies. The exact digital-shelf-label
package therefore remains **AMBER until Walmart gives written permission**.

## Priority Source Packages

### A. Walmart Digital Shelf Labels — AMBER

The June 6, 2024 press package contains eight named stills plus:

- `B-roll: Digital Shelf Labels` — 3:49
- `Digital Shelf - Associate Story` — 1:59

It is unusually valuable because it shows the exact Walmart implementation, including associates in
the aisle, scanning labels, online-order picking, stock-to-light, pick-to-light, and a rollback label.
Request written permission using `WALMART-PERMISSION-REQUEST.md`. Do not download merely because the
page exposes download controls.

### B. Official Proceedings and Government Records — Preferred

- Senate Judiciary hearing, `Your Data, Their Profit: The Consumer Cost of AI Surveillance Pricing`,
  for an attributed witness/room beat in Scene 08.
- Senator Rosen's official Senate page and video questioning the FTC chair about surveillance pricing.
- New Jersey governor page and enacted bill for Scene 09.
- New York City Council page and official hearing feed for the proposed safeguards in Scene 09.
- FTC surveillance-pricing report for the decisive Scene 08 evidence sequence.

Use the originating government feed or document, not a broadcaster's recording of the same event.
Confirm the particular recording's provenance and any embedded third-party material in the rights log.

### C. Licensed Generic-Real Bed — GREEN After License Is Logged

Search and acquire distinct clips for:

- grocery shoppers entering, comparing prices, and checking out;
- workers stocking shelves, scanning products, picking online orders, and replacing paper tags;
- label printers, shelf strips, checkout screens, and price-mismatch interactions;
- produce handling, near-expiry products, markdown stickers, purchase, and food waste.

Prefer footage that visibly supports the exact action. Avoid empty aerials, generic technology hands,
anonymous server rooms, and people staring theatrically at phones.

## Permission and Fallback Gate

Production does **not** wait for Walmart to answer. Scenes 01, 02, 04, and 06–11 can be sourced from
GREEN/licensed material immediately. Scene 03 is the only scene whose ideal version materially
benefits from Walmart's exact operational footage. If permission is denied or unanswered, rebuild
that scene from CC label imagery, licensed worker footage, the company's public written claims, and
clearly authored feature annotations.

## Definition of Ready for Scene Construction

A scene may enter HyperFrames construction only when:

- every factual beat has a real source or an authored, source-backed visualization;
- each external asset has a rights tier and attribution record;
- AMBER material has a written use rationale or a GREEN replacement;
- the real-footage duration is sufficient to prevent graphics from becoming the dominant register;
- document passages and highlight phrases are mapped to the relevant VO words;
- no visual implies personalized in-store pricing, identified people, or conduct that the evidence
  does not establish.
