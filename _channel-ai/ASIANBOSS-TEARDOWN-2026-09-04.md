## 3. The collision you have to decide

Faceless is settled for Byrddynasty and the avatar is permanently dead — none of that is in question
and none of it is being re-proposed. But **this is a new channel**, and per your own standing note
the faceless rule scopes to Byrddynasty only. So this is genuinely open, and you should decide it
knowing what the data says.

- **Stay faceless.** Then you are not copying this format — you are taking its *research spine* and
  replacing its *delivery spine*. That substitution is untested at 42 minutes. Your conduit visual
  system was built and proven at ~10–16 min. Something has to do the connective work the face does,
  across four times the runtime.
- **Put yourself on camera.** Then the format ports as-is, and the "an engineer explains" credential
  from yesterday's Shape 1 finding (11.51× on a 15,000-sub channel) becomes visible rather than
  claimed.

I am not deciding this for you. But do not pick faceless by default and then wonder why a 42-minute
cut feels loose — the 56% is the answer to a problem you would then still have.

object.

shape — it is the shape.

## 5. What I'd take from this

1. **The format worth copying is not the street interview.** It is the 40-minute researched
   explainer, and Asian Boss's own numbers make that case at 5.3×.
2. **It converges with Lane A.** Yesterday's strongest shape — a credentialed practitioner running
   the arithmetic, 31–54 min — and Asian Boss's winning format are structurally the same video,
   measured independently on different channels. Two roads, one destination.
3. **AI is a viable subject inside it, unproven.** n = 2, one at 2.01×, one at 0.45×. The structural
   question won; the market-event question lost.
4. **Geography is a doorway, not a subject.** "Why *place* does *thing*" gives you an object and a
   stake for free — which is the one property everything measured this week rewards.
5. **The presenter question is the real decision**, and it is upstream of every title.

**Next, when quota resets:** finish the eight unrun probes and test the AI × geography crossing
directly (*where the world's chips come from*, *which countries own the internet*, *Africa's data
centers*) — that is the specific cell where your Lane A material and this format overlap.
