# Concreteness Checklist — competitor teardown → treatment gate

**Apply during PIPELINE Step 3 (Treatment / variety pass), every scene.** This is the operational
distillation of a 5-video teardown of **Logically Answered** (~915K subs, our closest faceless
tech/AI/business peer). Full analysis + head-to-head: `jarvis-private/reports/LAanalysis`.

## The finding that drives this doc
Our craft (bespoke HyperFrames, cinematic dossier grade) is **higher** than the competitor's
screenshot-and-marker style. We have already independently converged on their winning core
(highlighter-on-source, big number reveals, two-register palette, "everything breathes",
number-anchored VO). The **one real gap is CONCRETENESS & VARIETY**: they fill the screen with real,
recognizable, human things; we default to beautiful *abstractions* (a fiber-optic tunnel for
"infrastructure", an atmospheric backdrop for a company). Abstract-only is the pattern to break.

## The per-scene concreteness pass (ask these before authoring)
For every scene, before you reach for a HyperFrames abstraction:

1. **Is there a REAL image of the actual thing?** The actual person's face, the real building, the
   real product/app UI, the real headline. If yes, show it (or show it *alongside* the design), not a
   symbol for it. A viewer should see the *thing*, not only our design of the idea.
2. **Is there a HUMAN in this beat?** If a person is quoted, named, or central — use a **source-card
   quote** (circular avatar + name/title + quote typing in, VO-synced) or a 3–5s **real interview/
   archival clip** of them speaking. Faceless video starves for human presence; put it back here.
3. **Is a real document being referenced?** Use the ACTUAL screenshot (SEC filing, article, tweet,
   filing) with a highlighter sweep on the load-bearing line — not a re-typed/designed lookalike.
4. **Is this an abstract number/quantity?** Keep converting it to data-viz (we already do this well) —
   but consider a **traveling-dot line chart** to the current value for "the stock did X" beats.
5. **Could a real photo carry this instead of a card?** If so, annotate it — **callout box + arrow**
   tagging the person/object makes a real photo as information-dense as a designed card.

**Target mix:** no more than ~2–3 consecutive scenes on pure abstraction/atmosphere. Spread real
imagery (faces, headlines, product/UI, interview clips) across the runtime the way
`PRESENTATION-VARIETY.md` spreads registers. Missing real assets → the GAP LIST (Step 3 / ASSET-GEN).

## Transferable components to reach for (from the teardown)
- **Source-card quote** — avatar + name/title + typed quote, VO-synced. Their highest-ROI move; the
  fastest way to add human presence to a faceless build. *(Generator candidate: `make-quote-card.py`,
  mirroring `make-dispatch-endcard.py`.)*
- **Real-photo callout** — box + curved arrow labeling people/objects in a real news photo.
- **Real screenshot + highlighter** — we already do the highlighter; the upgrade is using the REAL
  page, not a designed facsimile.
- **One-word beat card** — "Desperation." / "Way worse." — staccato pacing between serif landing lines.
- **Real interview/archival clip** — 3–5s of the subject actually speaking, as a breather + authenticity.

## Guardrails (do NOT copy these from the competitor)
- **Real, never fabricated.** 2 of the competitor's 5 videos used AI-fabricated "articles" and a fake
  "leaked recording." That is a credibility bomb. Real receipts beat fabricated ones — this is where
  we are *better*, not just different. Only ever screenshot a source that actually exists.
- **Keep our craft.** Do not drop to screenshot-and-marker; the bespoke graphics are our
  differentiator. The move is **premium dossier + their concreteness**, not a swap.
- **Keep our tighter beat.** Their long text-dense holds (10–14s) are slower than our 3–5s standard.
  Keep ours.

## One-line rule
**Show the thing, not only the idea of the thing — and make the thing real.**
