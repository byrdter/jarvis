## Editorial Promise

**Every video takes one specific claim about AI that somebody is currently making with money riding on
it, and works out whether the arithmetic holds.**

A method, not a topic — which is why it survives the proven AI subject set being only five things
wide (jobs, the bubble, slop, data centers, cognition). Each is a *site* for the instrument.

## The Four Commitments

1. **The object, never the category.** Every video is a named thing with a date. Ten measurements say
   the category has no audience — not a small one, none. (`AI is making decisions about your life`
   0.00× · `the end of the open web` 0.19× · `why digital transformation fails` 0.02% at 0% drift.)
2. **Show the work, and show it failing.** The arithmetic happens on screen — numbers, source, and the
   step where it breaks. Redhawk Media wins at 6.78× on 4,800 subs by *asserting* the lie without ever
   demonstrating it; the position below that is open.
3. **Publish the boring verdict.** When a claim checks out, that video ships too. This is what buys
   every other verdict its credibility, and nobody in the space does it.
4. **Practitioner, never professor.** Lead with what you can work out, never what you know or where
   you learned it. Measured: *"an engineer explains"* **11.51×** · *"a professor explains"* **0.01×** ·
   *"thirty years in tech"* **0.01×**. **Credential in the channel name is fine** (Leo Cui, Ph.D., CFA
   → 13.05×); credential in the hook is what dies.

## Topic and Demand Gate

Probe the **shape family**, never the drafted title — the tool returns INCONCLUSIVE on real titles and
cannot adjudicate between two. Proven shapes for this channel:

| Shape | Evidence |
|---|---|
| "The math behind [claim] is wrong" | 26.72× / 23.73× / 19.33×, all on channels under 15,000 subs |
| "The real reason behind [action]" | 51.59× on 7,140 subs · 45.34× on 29,500 |
| Direct accusation | 9.27× on 5,460 subs |
| The quiet reversal | 81.77× max |
| Atlas / "Every X explained" | 13.05× at 40.3 min · 11.51× on 57,500 |

**Dead as titles — do not use:** "we read the report so you don't have to" (n=0) · "the press release
versus the filing" (84% drift) · "nobody fact-checks" (0.01×) · "the adoption numbers are fake" (n=0)
· any prediction-scoring framing · any category framing.

**And the aiming rule:** point the debunk at the **companies' optimistic promise** (PROVEN 26.72×),
never at a pessimistic statistic (95%-of-AI-projects-fail = DEAD 0.03×) and never at the audience's
own anxiety (the doom rows win instead). **Anxiety is the demand.**

## Packaging Standard

- **Two-clause titles — fact, then withheld consequence.** Snap Shift: **3.86× views, p<0.0001.**
- Accusation frame where earned: named actor + adverse present-tense verb (**+1.69× reach**; raises
  the floor, not the ceiling).
- Information-first cold open — first frame carries a concrete readable thing. Never a dark abstract.
- Thumbnails carry Terry's face (PIPELINE Step 8b) — now literally true rather than an exception.

## VO and Retention Standard

- Verdict lands **past 40% of runtime** (`tools/narrative-measure.py`). The *AI Doesn't Hallucinate*
  master failed this at 5.6% — a real failure mode, not a theoretical one.

  first-person-plural rule was a faceless-channel artifact.
