# Episode Command-Center Templates

Copy these files into a new `video-XX-name/` folder at episode kickoff, then load the active channel
profile. For KeyAdvances, use `references/channel-profiles/KEYADVANCES.md`.

The templates define the operating surface for demand validation, the pre-script title/thumbnail gate,
research, script, visual treatment, assets, scenes, QC, unlisted upload review, localization, publishing,
and measurement. Complete the pre-script sections of `YOUTUBE-PACKAGE.md` before full research or
production; the publishing sections are completed after the final cut.
