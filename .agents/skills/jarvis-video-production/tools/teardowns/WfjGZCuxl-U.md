## ANALYSIS — filled 2026-07-31

### VERDICT: The strongest produced-faceless outlier measured — and the SHORTEST. 8:12.

39,500 subs · 2.88M views · **73.03×** · **8:12** · 151 wpm · 1,239 words. No borrowed authority,
no on-camera person, no interview. Everything here is reproducible by us.

**1. Hook shape.** **Public-reversal** (§7.4) — a named prophecy, then the receipts. Not a ceiling
claim and not a mechanism explainer.

**2. Fact-vs-meaning sort (§3).** Extraordinarily fact-dense. **Eight concrete data points in the
first 44 seconds:** 2023 · "up to 80% of software developers" · by 2025 · 152,000 laid off · Q1 2025
· Intel · Amazon · 30,000 roles. **GIVES** the prophecy, the numbers, and the layoffs. **WITHHOLDS**
whether the layoffs were actually caused by AI working — which is the whole video.

**3. Paradox test.** Passes: *the industry did the layoffs the prophecy called for, and the prophecy
was wrong anyway.* The viewer cannot resolve which of those two facts explains the other.

**4. Loop named at [1:09]** — and here the video **breaks the standard and survives**:
*"Today, we're going to look at how the plan to replace developers with AI went horribly and
predictably wrong."* That is §2's DELETE FOREVER register verbatim. **But it lands at 1:09, after a
69-second open that already did the work.** The open earned the right to be boring for one line. Do
not read this as permission to open that way.

**5. Reversal at [4:12]** — authored, and the best of the four teardowns:

complaint (bad code) to a human one (the junior death spiral, entry-level hiring down ~50%). Lands at
51% of runtime — inside §1's 40–55% window.
