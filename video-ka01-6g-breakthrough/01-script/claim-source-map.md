## Required softening

- Scenes 05, 08, 09 and 11 contain synthesis or applications, not promised products. Keep `could`, `plausible`, `evidence-based adjacency`, and `defensible order` language.
- `IT CAN SEE` is thumbnail compression. The VO and first payoff frame explicitly correct it to `IT CAN INFER`.
- The final order of arrival is our forecast and must display `OUR EVIDENCE-BASED FORECAST`.
