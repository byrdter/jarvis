## What Changes From V1

- Scene boundaries are not pauses. Each boundary is a 4–8-frame motion bridge, match cut, mask wipe, or object carry. No fade-to-background, held tail, or visible 0.7-second gap.
- Full-screen imagery is a primary register. Approximately 40% of the episode should be full-bleed footage or photographic stills, 30% designed HyperFrames, 20% evidence/diagrams, and 10% transitional or atmospheric material.
- Small inset footage is reserved for genuine evidence, comparisons, and supporting examples. It is not the default treatment for B-roll.
- Generated stills are preferred when the VO names a precise everyday situation that no existing clip depicts exactly. A still receives a controlled push, crop travel, depth separation, annotation, or progressive reveal; it is never simply parked on screen.
- Every named thing appears when it is spoken. General network imagery cannot stand in for a smart-glasses, fall-detection, fishing-boat, hospital-equipment, or employee-tracking beat.
- The Impact Map persists as a recurring story object, but it retracts completely during full-screen human moments. It returns only when a candidate changes position or the rules change.

Transition rules:

- Audio remains continuous. Do not overlap spoken words with `acrossfade`.
- The outgoing object remains visible until the incoming frame has recognizable content.
- Use directional continuity: a leftward exit must produce a leftward entrance; a push-in must resolve as a push-in.
- Blur/whip movement may hide no more than 4 frames. Decorative transition effects must not delay the next idea.
- Transition motion is authored inside the master HyperFrames timeline, not added as a slow assembly effect afterward.

## V2 Acceptance Gates

- No scene boundary contains more than 0.08 seconds of unmotivated visual or audio space.
- Boundary review at ±0.5 seconds shows continuous content and direction at all eleven seams.
- At least one full-screen photographic or footage beat appears in S00–S10; S11 is graphics-only.
- No B-roll appears in a small inset unless it is evidence, a comparison, or a deliberately secondary example.
- Every named everyday example is represented by the correct noun/action at its spoken timestamp.
- Still holds receive a meaningful crop move, depth move, annotation, or image change before five seconds.
- Full-screen clips play once and exit before looping.
- All future applications carry the correct maturity badge.
- The final master passes scene-validator, layout/occlusion checks, freeze/white-frame scans, and a dedicated eleven-boundary seam contact sheet.
