# Visual Treatment Board

The viewer should follow the argument muted. Final VO will be written to these actions.

## Visual Grammar

- **Persistent object:** Five-node 6G Impact Map with a human at its center. Visible continuously; expanded at every scene boundary.
- **Evidence register:** Cream source cards for papers, standards, dates, methods, and exact figures.
- **Analysis register:** Dark navy maps, schematics, comparisons, and forecasts. Gold marks demonstrated facts; teal marks systems; red appears only for a limit or risk.
- **Maturity layer:** DEMONSTRATED / STANDARD TARGET / PLAUSIBLE / SPECULATIVE badge on every future-facing application.
- **Density:** 45–60 change events per minute, driven primarily by within-beat motion rather than cuts.
- **Five-second ceiling:** No visual state remains unchanged for five seconds. Author a meaningful change every 2–4 seconds and verify every scene plus the assembled master with `freezedetect`.
- **Per-scene asset floor:** A different real background plus at least three single-use clips in every scene. See `MOTION-ASSET-MAP.md` for the concrete sequence and sourcing plan.
- **Denotative budget:** At least 90%. The present plan is approximately 98% DEN.

## Visual Risks

| Scene | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| All | Persistent map becomes decorative clutter | Keep it at 8–12% frame width between expansions; each node change must correspond to a real narrative advancement |
| S00 | Viewer assumes all three numbers came from one result | Date/source color coding begins immediately and resolves explicitly in S05 |
| S01 | Weak sourcing or stale 5G anecdote | Select two dated measures before VO and stamp their dates |
| S04/S07 | Future imagery reads as documentary proof | Pixel/generated treatment plus permanent PLAUSIBLE badge |
| S05 | Source-card text becomes unreadable on mobile | Crop to one claim, one figure, one method detail per card; verify rendered pixels |
| S06 | Thumbnail promise overstates sensing | Show `SEE → INFER`; explain modality and limit in the same beat |
| S08 | Job claims outrun evidence | Keep category level until direct labor/investment sources are mapped |
| S09 | Privacy beat feels bolted on | Use the exact same RF path for benefit and risk, making it a consequence of the mechanism |
| S10 | Verdict sounds certain about timing | Use tiers and dependencies; label the ordering as an evidence-based forecast |
