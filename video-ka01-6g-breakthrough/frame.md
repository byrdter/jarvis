# KeyAdvances Frame System

The visual world is a restrained technical documentary: midnight-blue analysis surfaces, warm cream primary-source evidence, gold demonstrated facts, teal system behavior, and red used only for a real limitation or governance risk.

The core tension is laboratory precision versus ordinary human life. Frames should feel like an investigative exhibit whose diagrams are alive—not a software dashboard, holographic science fiction, or a generic neon technology commercial.

## Typography

- Montserrat 900 carries verdicts and large numeric evidence at 64–132 px.
- Montserrat 400 carries short explanatory text at 28–40 px.
- IBM Plex Mono 400/700 carries dates, sources, measurement labels, evidence states, coordinates, and persistent-map metadata at 20–28 px.
- Never use paragraphs as primary screen content. Show the claim, measurement, relationship, or contradiction the narration is currently resolving.

## Composition

- Use 96–140 px title-safe padding.
- Every scene has background, midground, and foreground depth with 8–10 authored elements.
- The persistent five-node Impact Map remains a small corner instrument until a scene advances it.
- Evidence cards use the warm paper surface and preserve visible source/date attribution.
- Generated future imagery always carries DEMONSTRATED, STANDARD TARGET, PLAUSIBLE, or SPECULATIVE state.
- Meaningful information changes every 2–4 seconds; no visual state may remain unchanged for five seconds.

## Avoid

- Cyan-purple gradients, floating glass cards, generic holograms, centered web-page stacks, identical card grids, tiny UI text, decorative circuit boards without narrative meaning, or red used merely for excitement.
- Treating illustrations as evidence or displaying a whole paper page too small to read.
- Reusing an outside clip or source card in multiple scenes.
