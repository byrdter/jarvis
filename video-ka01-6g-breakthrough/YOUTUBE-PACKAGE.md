### Originality Firewall

- Transferable structural moves: A named breakthrough, a hard number, an early lab reveal, a mid-video reinterpretation, and consequences translated into a viewer's life.
- Original research / synthesis / utility we add: Separate the 2022 370-GHz record from the 2025 photonic-chip paper; expose the 1.3-metre test and external equipment; connect the experiment to ITU's sensing, positioning, AI, and coverage requirements; rank consequences by likely deployment horizon.
- Wording, examples, conclusions, visuals, and packaging we must not import: No competitor phrasing, scene order, thumbnails, metaphors, or unsupported 206.25-Gbps conclusion. The supplied video is a lead, not a script source.

## Thumbnail Brief

- Core visual: One person at the center of a volumetric network map. Radio arcs, depth contours, and object outlines reveal that the environment—not merely the phone—is being sensed.
- Face/person included: yes; human scale is essential, but expression should be alert rather than theatrical.
- Text on thumbnail: **IT CAN SEE**
- Background: Dark real-world street, station, or room dissolving into a luminous spatial mesh.
- Color emphasis: Warm human skin against deep navy; cyan/teal sensing mesh; one restrained red detection point.
- Source assets: Original/generated human and environment; no borrowed competitor frames.
- Prompt / design notes: 16:9 cinematic editorial image; waist-up person, slightly off-center; network-generated depth map wrapping around body and nearby objects; visible ranging rays and point cloud; strong silhouette at mobile size; leave clean negative space for three-word text; no phone hero, no robot face, no generic globe, no tiny UI copy.
- How it complements rather than repeats the title: The title says speed is not the point; the thumbnail reveals the more provocative capability—ambient perception.
