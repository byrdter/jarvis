Three numbers are being sold as one Chinese 6G breakthrough: two hundred six point two five gigabits per second. A chip eleven millimetres long. And a wireless test that crossed about one point three metres. They point toward real Chinese research. They don't describe one result.

That doesn't make the breakthrough fake. But the wrong question would be whether your next phone downloads faster.

Five candidates are competing for our 6G Impact Map: speed, reach, intelligence, sensing, and coordination. Which raises the real question: which could change your life first—and what would it ask in return?
