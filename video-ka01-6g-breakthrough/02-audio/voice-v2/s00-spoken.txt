Three numbers are being sold as one Chinese 6G breakthrough. A speed of over two hundred gigabits per second — fast enough to download a full 4K movie in about a second. A chip smaller than your fingernail. And a wireless signal that traveled... about four feet.

All three are real. But they're not one result — and none of them is the part that will change your life.

So let's ask a better question than "will my phone download faster?" Six generations of mobile networks have changed how we live before. What will this one actually do for us — and what will it ask in return?

To keep that question honest, we're building one 6G Impact Map as we go. Five candidates are competing for the center: SPEED, REACH, INTELLIGENCE, SENSING, and COORDINATION. Every candidate has to earn its place by changing something in ordinary life. By the end, we'll put them in order — but one of them is going to move much closer to you than the headlines suggest.
