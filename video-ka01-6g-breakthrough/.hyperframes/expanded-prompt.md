# Expanded Production Prompt — KeyAdvances 6G

## Style Lock

Use `frame.md` exactly: background `#07131F`, foreground `#F4F1E8`, muted `#9BAEB8`, demonstrated gold `#D6A84B`, systems teal `#42B8AD`, risk red `#D65C59`, evidence paper `#EEE8DA`, paper ink `#20282B`. Montserrat is the statement voice; IBM Plex Mono is the evidence/instrument voice. The atmosphere is investigative technical documentary—real laboratory proof colliding with human consequence.

## Rhythm

`rapid contradiction → lived-context montage → continuous technical build → geographic expansion → routing escalation → REVERSAL PULLBACK → precise sensing reveal → coordinated vignette cascade → opportunity climb → quiet moral fork → controlled forecast landing → clean CTA`

Primary transition: 0.35–0.45 second directional push or blur-through for related argument steps. Accents: paper-edge wipe into evidence and a short radial/RF ripple at the S05→S06 sensing reveal. Hard cuts are reserved for the opening three-number collision and the four S07 vignettes. No transition may create a visual pause.

## Global Motion and Density

- Persistent five-node Impact Map: small at upper right, full-frame only when advanced. Nodes lock, open, reorder, and connect; it never floats as decoration.
- Background: localized teal/gold radial energy, low-contrast cartographic/grid texture, one slowly drifting RF contour, fine grain. All ambient cycles are finite and seek-safe.
- Foreground: scene code, date, evidence-state badge, one measurement rail, registration marks, and a 2–4 second changing micro-readout.
- Real or generated media always moves through a crop, push, annotation, map overlay, or evidence comparison. No static slideshow treatment.
- All host video/audio remains in `index.html`; scene HTML contains only shells, diagrams, labels, and overlays.
