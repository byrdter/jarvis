# Storyboard — V3 Rebuild

## S00 — cold open

The viewer enters a moving photonic experiment. The headline's three claims become three physical tests: a phone download, an 11 mm object measurement, and a four-foot beam. The artifacts then split apart to reveal the headline's hidden problem. A phone-speed question is displaced by a person. Network generations rush past. A spatial sensing contour introduces the benefit/cost tension. Five distinct visual behaviors appear one at a time and collapse into a small edge instrument. Sensing ends closer to the person than expected.

## S01–S04 — expansion

Everyday-life montages establish the lived-change test. The chip is explored through scale, lanes, and reliability—not panels. Reach is shown against physical obstructions and real locations. Intelligence is demonstrated through lightweight devices borrowing nearby compute.

## S05 — reversal

The visual camera literally pulls back from the tiny chip to the laboratory around it. Separate source artifacts appear on opposite physical planes; their numbers do not merge. This is the midpoint reversal and the strongest evidence sequence.

## S06–S09 — consequences

Radio reflections build a spatial model around people and places. Coordination becomes shared live maps across roads, warehouses, ports, and hospitals. Job and business uses are shown as workflows. The same sensing language then turns from help toward control through ownership, access, and consent.

## S10–S11 — verdict and action

The best prior visuals return in a faster ranked montage. The edge instrument reorders itself based on ordinary-life consequence. The CTA continues the channel world with moving future imagery and tactile subscribe, like, and bell actions synchronized to Steve's VO.
