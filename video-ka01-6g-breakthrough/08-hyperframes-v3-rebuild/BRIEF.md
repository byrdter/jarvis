# KeyAdvances 6G — V3 Rebuild Brief

- Workflow: `general-video`
- Flow: `automation`
- Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 16:9
- Runtime: 770.40 seconds using the approved Steve VO
- Opening look-lock: 49.60 seconds
- Audience: ordinary people curious about useful near-future changes
- Core message: China's 6G work is real, but faster phones are the least consequential part.
- Narrative spine: five candidates—speed, reach, intelligence, sensing, coordination—compete to matter most in ordinary life.
- Tone: fast, intriguing, comprehensible, evidence-led, human-scale.

## Near-hard visual gates

1. No naked text on an empty background. Words must live inside evidence, instruments, labels attached to objects, interfaces, or composited footage.
2. Plain rectangular text boxes are limited to one or two uses in the entire master.
3. Every scene has a real image or moving visual bed; backgrounds change with the subject of the VO.
4. No unchanged visual state lasts longer than five seconds. A meaningful object, camera state, evidence layer, or background must change.
5. The opening is an information sprint, never a title sequence. It begins inside the breakthrough, makes the claims physical, and leaves the human consequence unresolved.
6. Every beat has a presentation job, a distinct container, and a denotative visual match to the VO.

## Opening promise

Within 18 seconds the viewer sees the three claims, understands that they are real but separate, and learns that the most important consequence is not phone speed. The remaining opening turns that contradiction into the five-candidate investigation.
