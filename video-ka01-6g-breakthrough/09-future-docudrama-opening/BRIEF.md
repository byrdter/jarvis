# KeyAdvances 6G — Future-Docudrama Opening Proof

- Workflow: `general-video`
- Flow: `companion`
- Storyboard: `yes`
- Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 16:9
- Target runtime: 78–84 seconds
- Audience: ordinary adults curious about useful near-future changes
- Message: A future 6G network may recognize and act on a human emergency without a camera or wearable—but the same power implies that the network already knows intimate patterns of life.
- Carried question: Which part of 6G actually saved Elena—and what else did the network learn?
- Truth state: `2034 · DRAMATIZED FUTURE` is visible from frame one.

## Concept angle

Begin as intimate human drama, let the danger become clear before revealing the invisible network, then accelerate into help already moving. The last beat converts relief into unease by revealing that the system recognized the emergency only because it already knew Elena's normal movement.

## Visual identity

- Human world: warm amber practical light, natural skin, lived-in materials, restrained documentary photography.
- Network world: thin cyan contours, point-cloud reconstruction, amber trajectories; never generic hologram panels.
- Truth label: a small edge-anchored documentary marker, not a title card.
- Focal element: Elena through the fall; the ambulance only becomes the focal element after the network has inferred the emergency.
- Background treatment: always photographic and location-specific; no black or empty presentation background.

## Near-hard gates

1. No title sequence and no naked text frame.
2. No unchanged state longer than five seconds; each plate receives camera movement, object motion, an information reveal, or an accelerating transition.
3. The first four beats contain no visible technology. Human danger lands first.
4. No surveillance camera, wearable, medical pendant, or phone call triggers the response.
5. The sensing visualization emerges from the room and attaches to physical surfaces.
6. The ending asks the question; it does not award a winner among the five candidates.
